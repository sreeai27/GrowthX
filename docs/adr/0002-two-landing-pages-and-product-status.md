# Two landing pages and honest product status

The public site uses two audience-specific landing pages: `/hunar-os` for operators, buyers and investors, and `/kaam-saathi` for workers and demo visitors. `/` redirects to Hunar OS, both pages provide an operator/worker switch, and the Hunar OS primary demo action leads to KaamSaathi rather than creating a third general landing page. This keeps each page focused while preserving a clear path between the enterprise system story and the worker experience.

Hunar OS presents one connected flow: KaamSaathi, Studio and Trace are available surfaces; Connect and Graph are platform previews; Passport and Network are explicitly future. KaamSaathi gives TaskConfirm and Ride Replay equal card weight, followed by expandable coming-soon scenario previews, while TaskConfirm remains the release-blocking golden path. Every page follows `DESIGN.md`, with the master specification taking precedence where they conflict.
